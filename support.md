# Camp Timanous Staff App: Support



The Camp Timanous app is for Camp Timanous staff. It rings your iPhone for camp calls, lets you call families from the camp's number, plays the camp's voicemail, and has a searchable directory of campers and their parents.

## Signing in

- Tap **Sign in with Google** and pick your camp Google Workspace account. Personal Gmail accounts can't sign in.
- If the app says you aren't on the staff list, ask the camp office to add your address.
- Not camp staff? Tap **Not camp staff? Explore the demo** to look around with sample data.

## Calls don't ring my phone

- Open the app once after signing in so it can register for calls.
- Check that the app is allowed to use the microphone (Settings > Camp Timanous).
- Make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing calls.

## The directory looks out of date

On the Me tab, tap **Refresh now** under Directory. The office updates the directory from the camp's registration system.
